# Checklist — Dependency Management

Subject: Dependency Management

Elements are defined in `standards/` (OTT/OCS in `owasp-supply-chain.md`; NET/NFX in `lifecycle-dotnet.md`;
JDK/SPR in `lifecycle-java.md`; PY in `lifecycle-python.md`; NODE/EXP in `lifecycle-javascript.md`). Every element belongs to exactly one active check.
DEP-06 is retired: don't evaluate it or write it to `checkResults`, and never reuse the ID.
